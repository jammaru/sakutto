Workspace settings

These options apply to every project in this workspace. Changes are logged and take effect immediately for new runs; finished reports are not recalculated.

Data retention

Choose how long raw inputs are kept after a report is generated. Aggregated metrics are retained for two years regardless of this setting.

Keep raw inputs

Applies to uploaded files and copied text. Deleting an input does not delete the report that was already produced from it.

90 days

Keep intermediate results

Intermediate tables are kept so a failed step can be resumed without repeating the expensive upstream work.

14 days

Access and sharing

Members inherit access from the workspace role. Guest links expire automatically and can be revoked at any time from the sharing panel.

Default share link

New reports are created with this visibility. Owners can always tighten the setting on an individual report.

Members only

Require two-factor authentication

Members without a second factor keep their current session and are prompted at the next sign-in.

Enabled

Compute

Larger machines reduce wall-clock time for wide tables and shorten the queue at peak hours. Running reports are not migrated when this changes.

Default machine

Two cores are enough for most reports. Choose four for tables above ten million rows or for anything that joins several uploaded files.

Standard · 2 cores

Queue priority

Priority orders jobs inside the workspace, not against other workspaces, and does not guarantee a start time during an outage.

Normal
