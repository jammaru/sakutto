What the tide gauges recorded during the outage

The gauges kept recording through the three days when the reporting system was offline, so the record is complete even though nobody could read it at the time. A technician recovered the files on Thursday morning from a laptop that had been left running in the shed.

Each file covers six hours and holds a reading every ten seconds, which is finer than the old paper charts ever managed. The first surprise was the size of the short-period oscillation that appears whenever the wind drops below four knots.
