Osaka

Osaka (oːsaka; 大阪) is a designated city in Japan.[1]

By the Edo period, 大坂 (Ōsaka) was the commercial center of the region.

Universities include Osaka University, Osaka Metropolitan University, and Kansai University.

The city is a major financial center and the home of electronics companies such as Panasonic and Sharp.
